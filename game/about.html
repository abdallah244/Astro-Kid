THE CREW

MEET THE CREW

Three characters guide every mission: the kid in the suit, the robot beside you and the calm voice from Earth.

  1. THE PLAYER

    THE COMMANDER

    That is you. A kid in a white and orange suit with a blue visor, in charge of a two-person mission. You explore, collect, research, build and keep the crew alive.

    • ROLE The player
    • OUTFIT White and orange suit, blue visor
    • JOB Run the outpost, one choice at a time
  2. ROBOT GUIDE

    NOVA

    A small floating robot who helps you on site. Guide, tutor and the voice that explains how each system works, in short, simple sentences. Friendly and curious.

    • ROLE Guide and tutor
    • VOICE Bright and friendly
    • STYLE Short, simple sentences
  3. MISSION CONTROL

    DR. KARIM

    Your link to Mission Control on Earth, heard over the radio only. Calm, kind and practical. On the Moon his answers arrive almost at once; on Mars they take a long time, so you will have to decide alone.

    • ROLE Mission Control on Earth
    • VOICE Calm, with a radio filter
    • HEARD Over the radio only

HOW REGIONS OPEN

Every region is in one of three states. Exploring is what changes them.

  • 1 · OPEN

    WALK RIGHT IN

    Lunar Base and Lunar Plains are open from the start. Explore them freely.

  • 2 · FOG

    FIND IT

    Fogged regions show a "?" on the map. Walk in to discover one: its name appears, a "New area discovered!" message pops up and you earn a discovery bonus.

  • 3 · LOCKED

    EARN IT

    Locked regions show a padlock. They open as missions advance, and some need the right protection and equipment first, like water ice.

READING THE MAP

  • MINIMAP

    A round minimap sits in the top-right corner and follows you. When the outpost is far away, a little house arrow points the way back.

  • FULL MAP · KEY M

    Press M outside the outpost to open the full map. Discovered areas show full detail, the rest stay foggy, and a yellow pin shows where you are.

  • MAP KEY
    • Base: your outpost
    • You: your position
    • Cave: a dark cave to enter
    • Fog: not discovered yet
    • Locked: not available yet
INSIDE THE OUTPOST

FOUR SYSTEMS KEEP YOU GOING

Every mission starts at the lunar base. Four systems inside it look after the crew, and one of them is the doorway to the Technology Tree.

  • OPENS THE TECH TREE

    WORKSTATION

    Your research desk. Open it to see the Technology Tree and unlock new tools and machines.

  • SPARE GEAR

    SUPPLY STORAGE

    The crew's locker. It holds 3 oxygen canisters, 5 canned food, 4 spare batteries and 1 spare parts kit.

  • KEEPS YOU BREATHING

    LIFE SUPPORT

    A temporary life support reserve of about 72 hours. That number is a game setting, not real engineering.

  • RESTORES STAMINA

    SLEEPING AREA

    Sleep to restore stamina. Time keeps moving while you rest. It becomes available later in the mission.
