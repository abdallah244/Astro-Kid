HOW REGIONS OPEN

Every region is in one of three states. Exploring is what changes them.

  • 1 · OPEN

    WALK RIGHT IN

    Lunar Base and Lunar Plains are open from the start. Explore them freely.

  • 2 · FOG

    FIND IT

    Fogged regions show a "?" on the map. Walk in to discover one: its name appears, a "New area discovered!" message pops up and you earn a discovery bonus.

  • 3 · LOCKED

    EARN IT

    Locked regions show a padlock. They open as missions advance, and some need the right protection and equipment first, like water ice.

READING THE MAP

  • MINIMAP

    A round minimap sits in the top-right corner and follows you. When the outpost is far away, a little house arrow points the way back.

  • FULL MAP · KEY M

    Press M outside the outpost to open the full map. Discovered areas show full detail, the rest stay foggy, and a yellow pin shows where you are.

  • MAP KEY
    • Base: your outpost
    • You: your position
    • Cave: a dark cave to enter
    • Fog: not discovered yet
    • Locked: not available yet
