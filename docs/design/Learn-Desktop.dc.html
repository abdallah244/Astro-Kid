NOVA SAYS

Every spot on this map is a decision. Each one costs oxygen, power, time or tool durability.

  • 1LIFE SUPPORT

    Oxygen is limited. Make it, save it, or ship it from Earth.

  • 2POWER BUDGET

    Solar panels sleep at night, so batteries and storage decide who survives.

  • 3GROWING FOOD

    You cannot depend on Earth for every meal. Grow your own.

  • 4WATER & ICE

    Ice hides in shadowed polar regions and has to be found and recycled.

  • 5RADIO DELAY

    Earth cannot answer instantly, so the crew must decide for itself.

  • 6RESOURCES

    Rocks hold minerals, not pure metals. Learn where iron, titanium and silicon really come from.
