INSIDE THE OUTPOST

FOUR SYSTEMS KEEP YOU GOING

Every mission starts at the lunar base. Four systems inside it look after the crew, and one of them is the doorway to the Technology Tree.

  • OPENS THE TECH TREE

    WORKSTATION

    Your research desk. Open it to see the Technology Tree and unlock new tools and machines.

  • SPARE GEAR

    SUPPLY STORAGE

    The crew's locker. It holds 3 oxygen canisters, 5 canned food, 4 spare batteries and 1 spare parts kit.

  • KEEPS YOU BREATHING

    LIFE SUPPORT

    A temporary life support reserve of about 72 hours. That number is a game setting, not real engineering.

  • RESTORES STAMINA

    SLEEPING AREA

    Sleep to restore stamina. Time keeps moving while you rest. It becomes available later in the mission.

HOW A MACHINE IS MADE

  2. 2CRAFTGather the ingredients the recipe asks for and craft the item.
  3. 3INSTALLPlace the finished machine in the outpost with E.
MACHINE · FIRST ONE YOU BUILD

MATERIAL PROCESSOR

A simple machine that crushes and sorts lunar samples. You research it first under Tools, then craft it from rocks you collect on the surface and in the caves.

RECIPE
  • ×5Lunar Regolith
  • ×5Iron-bearing Rock
  • ×5Silicate Rock
  • ×1Spare Parts Kit

When it is ready, install it in the outpost with E. The processor has two looks in the game: off and running.
