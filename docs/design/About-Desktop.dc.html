ABOUT THE GAME

A KID. A FRAGILE OUTPOST.
THE MOON.

You are the young Commander of a two-person mission, arriving at a half-built outpost inspired by the Moon's south pole. Supplies are limited, the air is temporary and the power is not guaranteed. Explore, research, build and keep the crew alive.
