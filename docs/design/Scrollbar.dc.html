1. THE EDUCATIONAL, NON-PROFIT GAME

Astro Kid is a fully educational, non-profit project, developed independently to spread knowledge and offer a free, interactive learning experience to everyone.

The game and the website contain no sales, paid subscriptions or financial transactions of any kind, and we never ask you for any banking or financial data.

2. ADULTS AND CHILDREN

This game is designed to be suitable and useful for adults and children alike as general educational content. It is available to everyone, without complicated age restrictions.

Data collection on the website is limited to the minimum needed for registration and download. We do not ask for any sensitive personal data and we do not build profiles of users.

3. WHAT WE COLLECT

When you visit the website or register to download the game, we collect only the following categories of data: registration data, Google account data, your full name, basic technical data and communication data.

Your Internet Protocol (IP) address, your operating system type and version, and your browser type are used to make sure the game files are compatible with your device and to protect the website from cyberattacks.

4. SIGNING IN WITH GOOGLE

We use Google Sign-In only to identify you and create your account. Google does not give us your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

You can withdraw this website's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account settings.
