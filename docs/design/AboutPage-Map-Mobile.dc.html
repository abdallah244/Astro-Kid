The missions are linked: your base, supplies, research and discovered map carry over from one to the next. Follow the trail to see what you do and what you learn.

HOW EVERY MISSION WORKS
  1. 1

    EXPLORE

    Walk the surface and find new regions.

  2. 2

    COLLECT

    Mine rock samples with your pickaxe.

  3. 3

    RETURN

    Carry the samples back and store them.

  4. 4

    RESEARCH

    Unlock new technology at the workstation.

  5. 5

    BUILD

    Craft machines and install them in the base.

  6. 6

    SURVIVE

    Keep oxygen, stamina and hunger healthy.

  1. YOU DO

    Contact Earth, inspect the four base systems, explore the plains, collect rock samples and build your first machine.

    YOU LEARN

    Resources are limited, local materials matter, tools wear out, and no mineral is a pure metal.

  2. YOU DO

    Balance solar panels, batteries and which machines to run.

    YOU LEARN

    Power budgeting and storage, and why night is a problem on the Moon.

  3. YOU DO

    A solar storm is coming. Build and place shielding and decide what to power.

    YOU LEARN

    Space weather, shielding, and the cost of safety.

  4. YOU DO

    Build a growing area, analyze the soil and plant seeds.

    YOU LEARN

    Closed-loop food, what plants need, and why growing food in space is hard.

  5. YOU DO

    Find ice in a protected region, extract it and recycle your water.

    YOU LEARN

    Water is the most valuable resource, and how crews reuse it.

  6. YOU DO

    Run every system together for a full week of events, nights and choices.

    YOU LEARN

    Balancing everything at once: the real job of a mission planner.
